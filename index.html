The one idea behind all of these

Marketplace fees are charged on the order total — the item plus the shipping the buyer paid. That single fact breaks the arithmetic most sellers use, because raising your price to cover a fee also raises the fee.

If fees come to 11% and you want to clear $30, pricing at $30 plus 11% leaves you short every time. The fee lands on the higher number, not the one you started from. Netting a target after a percentage is taken out means dividing, not multiplying:

order total = (what you want to clear + costs) ÷ (1 − fee rate)

Every tool here runs that calculation properly. It's not complicated maths, but getting it backwards is the single most common reason a handmade product that looks profitable isn't.
